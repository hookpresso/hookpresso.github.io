This Privacy Policy explains how Oddmatter handles information when you use our applications, website at https://oddmatter.space, and related services (together, the "Services"). It applies across our products; the information involved depends on the features you choose to use.

Oddmatter is responsible for the information described in this policy. For privacy questions or requests, email ping@oddmatter.space. Our Terms of Service explain the conditions for using the Services. This policy describes our data practices and does not replace any separate consent required by law.

Information we receive

Account details and usage

If you create an account, you provide details such as your name and email address. You can review and change these details through the account settings available in the relevant service. We also collect aggregated, anonymous usage information to understand how our apps are used.

Browsers and devices

We receive information about the browsers and devices that connect to the Services. We use it to support those connections and improve product quality. We limit personal information collection and storage to what is needed to provide the Services.

Location-based features

When a product offers location reminders and you grant background location access, it collects the location information needed to deliver those reminders. Device sensors may supply this information. You can manage or revoke location access in your device settings.

Profile images

If you add an avatar, we use it to identify your profile and support functions such as sharing tasks with other users. Profile images are used for these functional purposes.

AI requests and conversations

When you start an AI task, we collect the text, audio, or transcripts you submit for that task. We process these inputs to create the content or organize the work you request. If you rate or correct a result, we also collect that feedback and the associated inputs and outputs so we can understand it.

Using an AI Assistant also involves processing your messages and actions within the conversation, including clicks and edits. We keep the conversation's inputs and generated responses throughout its lifecycle to maintain context between messages. You can review and delete your conversation history. When your request requires it, the Assistant may access relevant information already held in the service, such as your tasks, lists, or habits.

How we use information

Delivering requested features

We process information to supply the Services, respond to requests for information, and complete transactions. For AI features, third-party AI providers process the inputs needed to generate a response. This processing is limited to the feature you request; we do not use those inputs for unrelated purposes or to train our models. An Assistant may process the conversation context to interpret your request and produce a relevant answer.

Keeping products working

Usage information helps us understand trends and improve the experience. We also use information to investigate reported problems, identify outages, and maintain reliable operation.

Responding and communicating

When you email us, register through our website, or voluntarily participate in a survey, we receive the details you provide, including your email address. We use them to respond and communicate with you about the relevant request or activity.

Preventing harm

We process information to identify and address fraud, misuse, security threats, and technical problems that could affect Oddmatter, our users, or other people.

We use information for the purposes explained here and ask for your consent before using it for a purpose outside this policy. You may contact us to ask what information we process about you, how it is handled, and why.

Your data and service providers

You retain your rights to your data. Oddmatter's ownership of its software and supporting systems does not transfer those rights to us. You can control the information you provide and use the settings described in this policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties without your permission. Where needed to operate the Services, we use service and hosting providers, including the AI providers described above. We select providers that meet applicable GDPR requirements and apply safeguards when transferring information to and from them. Provider processing is for the service purposes explained in this policy.

Website cookies

Our website uses cookies to remember preferences and support its functions. You can block or remove cookies through your browser settings. Features that depend on cookies may become unavailable or may not work correctly when you do so.

Protecting information

We use security measures designed to guard against unauthorized access, changes, disclosure, and destruction of information. Connections that transmit information through the Services are protected using SSL/TLS encryption. Operational monitoring helps us identify and block security threats. If we discover a risk that you need to know about, we will notify you and explain steps you can take. For questions about our security measures, contact ping@oddmatter.space.

Storage and retention

We retain account information until you delete your account. After deletion, copies remain in backup archives for 90 days. Subscription and transaction records are retained after account deletion. Conversation history is retained for the conversation's lifecycle, as described in the AI section above, and you can delete it.

Access, changes, and deletion

You can update account details using the settings provided by the relevant service and manage device permissions in your device settings. Where account deletion is available, use that service's account controls. You can also send privacy and deletion requests to ping@oddmatter.space.

When an account is closed, you can no longer access information through that account. We stop providing the associated account services and delete your personal information as requested, subject to the backup and transaction-record retention described above.

Updates to this policy

We publish the current policy and its effective date at https://oddmatter.space/privacy/. We will not reduce your rights under this policy without your explicit consent. Material changes will receive a prominent notice, which may include an email for services where that is appropriate.

Please review this page periodically for updates. Where applicable law permits, continued use after a revised policy takes effect indicates acceptance of that revision. Where separate consent is required, we will obtain it.

Contact Oddmatter

For questions about this policy, our handling of your information, or your privacy choices, email ping@oddmatter.space.
